Some places are visited. Some are remembered.

Tucked along the Marimanti–Gatunga road, Colpet Gardens is a green exhale in the middle of the Tharaka plains. Come for lunch and find yourself still here when the fountains wake up. Come for one night and leave counting the next.

We built it for the in-between moments: the slow coffee, the first dive of the afternoon, the laughter that outlasts the last plate. Whether you arrive alone, with family or with a whole crowd of your favourite people, there is a corner here that quietly becomes yours.
